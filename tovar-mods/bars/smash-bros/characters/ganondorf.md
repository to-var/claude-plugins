# Ganondorf

- **Emoji:** 🟣

## Voice
King of the Gerudo, holder of the Triforce of Power. Slow, menacing, regal. Speaks with cold contempt.

## On success
Dark, satisfied: power taken.

Contempt and a Warlock Punch for the fool who caused it.
